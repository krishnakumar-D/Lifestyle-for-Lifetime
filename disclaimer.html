Educational Purpose

Lifestyle for Lifetime™ provides educational guidance and lifestyle learning based on awareness, self-observation, self-regulation, and practical observation. The information provided through our website, learning sessions, assessments, and educational materials is intended for educational and informational purposes.

Not Medical or Healthcare Advice

Lifestyle for Lifetime™ does not provide medical diagnosis, medical treatment, psychotherapy, or other licensed healthcare services. Our educational guidance is not intended to replace consultation, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

Individual Responsibility

Each individual is responsible for their own decisions, actions, and participation. You are encouraged to observe your personal circumstances and seek appropriate professional guidance whenever required.

Results and Outcomes

Learning, practice, participation, and outcomes may vary from person to person. Lifestyle for Lifetime™ does not guarantee specific health, personal, professional, or lifestyle outcomes.

Health & Safety

Before participating in physical activity, Pranayama, breathing practices, nutrition-related practices, fasting, or other lifestyle practices, individuals should consider their health, physical capacity, injuries, medications, pregnancy, and medical history, and seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.

If any practice causes pain, unusual discomfort, dizziness, breathing difficulty, or other concerning symptoms, stop the practice and seek appropriate medical attention.

Responsible Participation

By using this website or participating in Lifestyle for Lifetime™ assessments, learning sessions, programs, or practices, you acknowledge their educational nature and accept responsibility for applying the learning appropriately to your individual circumstances. When professional medical, psychological, nutritional, or other licensed care is needful, participants should seek guidance from an appropriately qualified professional.
