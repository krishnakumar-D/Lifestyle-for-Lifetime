FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you begin.

Practical answers about beginning, learning formats, participation, questions and support.

1. Do I need any previous yoga, fitness, or wellness experience to begin?

No. You begin from your present lifestyle and learn according to what is needful for you.

2. Is Lifestyle for Lifetime™ a fixed program for everyone?

No. The learning is guided according to your present lifestyle, individual needs, and what requires attention.

3. Do I need to be physically fit to begin?

No. You begin from your present condition, and the learning is adapted according to what is appropriate and needful for you.

4. Can I participate if I have a health condition or am receiving medical treatment?

Yes, where appropriate. Please disclose relevant health conditions and ongoing treatment before beginning. Lifestyle for Lifetime™ education does not replace medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare.

5. How does the learning begin?

It begins with a Lifestyle Clarity Assessment™ to recognise your present needs and determine the most appropriate learning program for you.

6. How much time do I need to dedicate?

You will need to attend your scheduled learning sessions at the available timings. The number and duration of sessions depend on the learning program you choose. Participation is limited so that each individual receives dedicated attention. Daily observation and practice continue naturally as part of your everyday life.

7. Are the learning sessions online or in person?

Learning sessions can be conducted online or in person, depending on availability and the learning program selected.

8. What happens after the Lifestyle Clarity Assessment™?

The assessment helps us recognise what is needful in your present lifestyle. From there, the appropriate learning program and session schedule are arranged according to the availability of both the participant and the educator.

9. Can the program be adjusted as I progress?

Yes. As you progress, we review what is working for you and what needs more attention, and adjust the learning accordingly.

10. Can I ask questions between scheduled sessions?

Yes. You are always welcome to ask questions whenever you need clarity. There is no fixed limit on the number of questions you can ask during your learning journey.

11. Can I continue learning after completing a program?

Yes. You can continue with further learning according to your interests, needs, and the programs available at that time.

12. What kind of learning and practical activities will I have?

Your learning may include Yoga Asanas, Pranayama, Yoga Philosophy, Yoga Mudras, Chakra Meditation, Energy Awareness & Self-Regulation, physical fitness and flexibility, nutrition awareness and mindful eating, stress management, meditation, reflection and journaling, sleep, habits, relationships, and daily planning. You may also learn a practical day-to-day protocol aligned with the circadian clock, according to your individual needs and the program you choose.

13. Will I receive anything to follow between sessions?

Yes. Depending on your program, you may receive daily practices, reflections, learning activities, or simple guidelines to follow between sessions. These help you apply what you learn in your everyday life.

14. Will I receive support throughout the program?

Yes. Support continues throughout your learning period through scheduled sessions, follow-ups, and communication whenever you need clarity.

15. Who can participate in Lifestyle for Lifetime™ programs?

Lifestyle for Lifetime™ welcomes people of all ages, genders, abilities, and backgrounds, including people with disabilities. Individuals, couples, families, and groups can participate, with learning adapted where appropriate to individual needs, abilities, and available schedules.
