Do You Know How to Breathe? And What Is Guiding Your Thoughts?

Learning the breath is not the destination. It is the beginning of learning about yourself.

← What Is Your Present Lifestyle Providing You?

Every moment of your life, you breathe. You breathe while thinking, speaking, working, eating, feeling, resting and fulfilling your responsibilities.

But how often have you consciously observed your own breath? Before trying to regulate it, begin by observing its natural movement.

Now consider a thought. What need, memory, feeling or circumstance may be influencing it? Does this thought need action, acceptance, or simply observation?

A thought may influence a decision. A decision may become an action. Repeated actions can gradually shape the way we live. This is a practical invitation to self-observation.

Breath → Thought → Need → Decision → Action → Direction

At Lifestyle for Lifetime™, we connect awareness of present reality with relevant learning, practical guidance and conscious participation in everyday life.

Before your next action, ask: Is this action guided by what is needful in my present reality?
