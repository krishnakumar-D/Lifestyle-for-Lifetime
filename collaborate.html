COLLABORATE WITH PURPOSE

Contribute your knowledge. Strengthen practical human development.

If your professional expertise, lived experience or creative work can contribute meaningfully to responsible learning, we welcome a conversation about how it might fit.

Our mission begins with personalised lifestyle coaching and human performance training, and includes the development of wider learning resources. A contribution proposal is not an application for automatic employment, partnership, certification or authority to represent our organisation.

WHERE CONTRIBUTIONS MAY FIT

Different capabilities. One clear responsibility.

We consider ideas according to the needs of participants, relevance to the K-Lifestyle Framework™, feasibility and appropriate professional boundaries.

01

Professional expertise

Relevant yoga, physical training, nutrition, behaviour, well-being or other appropriately qualified specialised knowledge that complements existing guidance.

02

Learning & methodology

Curriculum development, educational writing, accessible learning methods, responsible research proposals and practical review.

03

Community & organisations

Potential projects with communities, institutions and organisations addressing meaningful human development needs.

04

Creative & technical work

Translation, design, video, technology, documentation and other skills that make learning clearer, more accessible and more useful.

SUITABILITY & PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES

How collaboration can begin responsibly.

We do not promise an available position, paid work, business partnership or use of contributed material simply because someone contacts us.

  1. Share a focused proposal. Explain what you want to contribute, its value and your relevant experience.
  2. Review scope and evidence. We consider relevance, qualifications, relevant licensing, references where needed, risks, resources and readiness.
  3. Agree the arrangement. Any project needs a written understanding of responsibilities, compensation (if any), confidentiality, intellectual property, brand permissions, review and termination.
  4. Begin only when authorised. No person may present themselves as our employee, certified practitioner, affiliate or official representative without explicit written approval.

Clinical treatment, diagnosis, medication decisions and other regulated professional services are outside the scope of our non-pharmacological lifestyle coaching unless independently provided within a properly qualified practitioner's lawful professional role and separately agreed boundaries.

PROPOSE A MEANINGFUL CONTRIBUTION

What can you contribute to the lives of others?

Write a short introduction, your area of expertise or practical contribution, relevant experience, proposed scope and preferred way to discuss it. Please do not send sensitive participant information in an initial enquiry.

Proposals are reviewed individually; submitting one creates no contractual relationship. Personal information is handled according to our Privacy Policy, subject to applicable requirements.
