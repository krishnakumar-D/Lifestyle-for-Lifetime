Terms & Conditions

Important information for understanding your participation and communication with Lifestyle for Lifetime™.

Educational Nature of Services

Lifestyle for Lifetime™ provides educational guidance focused on awareness, self-observation, self-regulation, lifestyle learning, and mindful living. Our services are educational in nature and are not a substitute for medical diagnosis, treatment, psychotherapy, or other licensed professional healthcare.

Participation & Personal Responsibility

Learning requires active participation, observation, reflection, and appropriate action by the individual. Each person remains responsible for their own decisions, actions, lifestyle choices, and application of the education provided. Individual observations and outcomes may vary.

Health & Medical Responsibility

Participants are responsible for informing Lifestyle for Lifetime™ of relevant health conditions, injuries, medications, medical advice, or physical limitations before participating in practices. Where appropriate, participants should consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning or regulating physical activity, breathing practices, nutrition, or other lifestyle practices.

Appointments & Communication

Learning sessions are provided by appointment. Participants are expected to attend scheduled sessions on time and communicate responsibly regarding availability, rescheduling, or cancellation. Communication through WhatsApp, phone, email, or other available channels is intended for appropriate educational communication related to the participant’s learning journey.

Fees, Payments & Cancellations

Fees for assessments, learning sessions, programs, and other services will be communicated before scheduling. Payment confirms the booking and reserves the agreed time for the participant. Once payment has been made and the assessment, session, or program has been scheduled, the fee is non-refundable.

If a participant needs to reschedule, Lifestyle for Lifetime™ must be informed at least 24 hours before the scheduled time. The session may then be rescheduled according to mutual availability.

A cancellation or rescheduling request made less than 24 hours before the scheduled time, or failure to attend the scheduled session, will be treated as the session having been reserved and the fee will not be refunded or transferred to another session.

Lifestyle for Lifetime™ respects the participant's time and commits its time according to every confirmed schedule. Participants are expected to respect the same commitment.

If Lifestyle for Lifetime™ is unable to conduct a scheduled session, an alternative date and time will be arranged without loss of that session.

Acceptance of Terms

By booking, enrolling in, accessing, or participating in a Lifestyle for Lifetime™ assessment, learning session, program, or service, you acknowledge that you have read and accepted these Terms & Conditions and agree to participate responsibly. Lifestyle for Lifetime™ may regulate its services, learning structure, schedules, or participation requirements when reasonably necessary while respecting the educational purpose of the program.
